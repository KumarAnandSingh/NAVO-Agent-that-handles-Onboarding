# Relocation support

<!-- Synthetic sample policy for the Navo demo. Halden is a fictional company. -->

## Who is eligible

Joiners who move more than 100 km to take up their role are eligible for relocation support. The offer letter states whether relocation applies.

## What Halden pays for

Halden reimburses economy travel for the joiner and their family, and the cost of moving household goods up to ₹75,000. Receipts are submitted through the expenses tool within 30 days of joining.

## Temporary housing

Eligible joiners can stay up to 14 nights in a Halden partner hotel near the office while they find a home. Navo books the stay once HR confirms eligibility.

## How to claim

Ask Navo or your HR onboarding owner to start a relocation request. HR approves it within 2 working days, and Navo tracks each step until the money is paid.
